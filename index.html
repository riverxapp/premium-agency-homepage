The problem

Most agencies sell you a pretty picture. We deliver a system that holds up.

You've been here before. A beautiful deck, a big promise, and a launch that's slow, brittle, and quietly expensive to maintain. Handoffs break. Timelines slip. Nobody can tell you what the work actually moved.

We built FATHOM around a different assumption: craft and performance aren't a trade-off — they're the same discipline. Every design decision is paired with a number, every number with an owner, and every project with one senior accountable from first sketch to final deploy.

No account managers relaying messages. No juniors learning on your budget. Just the people doing the work, showing it to you every week.
